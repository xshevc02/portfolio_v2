About me

The interface is the smallest part of the problem.

Underneath every screen I work on there are domain rules, technical constraints, expert habits and engineering decisions — and all of them shape what “good UX” even means here.

So I start with the logic of the workflow: what someone is trying to decide, which data they rely on, where a mistake becomes expensive. Then I make the complicated action feel clearer without pretending it is simple.

Day to day that means sitting with Application Specialists and domain experts, asking a lot of questions, and testing whether my answer survives contact with a real user.
